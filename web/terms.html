Portfolio demonstration

Nextrole is a software portfolio project for learning and internship evaluation. Company names and sample job descriptions demonstrate interface behavior and do not imply affiliation, endorsement, or a verified vacancy. No hiring, salary, or interview outcome is promised. Last updated: 17 October 2026.

Accounts

Browse without registering. A confirmed account is required to submit applications or manage private files. New accounts are candidates. Employer access requires administrator approval. Administrator roles are assigned through server administration, never public registration. Keep credentials secure and use information you are entitled to provide.

Acceptable use

Do not upload unlawful material, malware, another person's personal information, or confidential documents. Do not attempt to access other accounts or disrupt the service. Use sample data when evaluating the portfolio.

Applications and uploads

Saving a private draft does not submit it. Deliberate submission shares the reviewed name, email, cover note and a fixed copy of the specifically selected PDF version with the company for that job. Employers may update candidate-visible status and retain private notes. Library uploads are PDF only, up to 5 MB each, with up to 10 résumés and 50 private versions. Use sample information to evaluate the portfolio. No verified vacancy or hiring outcome is promised.

Availability and changes

This demonstration may be updated, reset, or discontinued. It is not suitable as the only record of an application or résumé. Features and account services depend on their deployment configuration.

The Privacy Policy and Retention Policy describe this portfolio’s handling of information. Contact its operator through the app’s private privacy-request channel. The operator does not present the portfolio as a registered recruitment business or promise jurisdiction-specific compliance. Nothing in these terms limits rights that applicable law does not allow to be limited. A commercial recruitment launch requires a separate operator and jurisdiction review.
