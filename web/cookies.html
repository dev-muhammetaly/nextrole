Privacy & Storage Preferences

Essential storage

When you sign in, Supabase stores a session so you remain signed in. A job identifier can be stored to resume your application flow after authentication. We remember your privacy choice in browser storage. These support functionality you request.

Optional storage

No optional analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts are installed. Accepting optional storage currently enables no additional tracking. Rejecting it does not prevent browsing or signing in.

Change your choice

On the website, use the Privacy settings button to reopen the preference notice. You may also clear the site's browser storage to remove the preference and local session. Signing out ends the current app session.
