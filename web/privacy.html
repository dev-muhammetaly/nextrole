NEXTROLE / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Open private privacy requests

Who operates Nextrole

Nextrole is maintained by its portfolio project owner as an internship demonstration. It is not presented as an operating recruitment agency or an identity-verification service. Contact the operator through Privacy & contact in the app. No personal email address is published. This notice covers this portfolio service. Last updated: 17 October 2026.

Information you provide

An account uses your name, email and Supabase-managed credentials. You may add profile information, private résumé PDFs, application drafts, contact details and cover notes. Employer accounts provide company information and job listings. The app also stores submitted applications, status events, bookmarks, in-product notices, reports, administrative decisions and privacy requests. Use fictional or sample information to evaluate this portfolio; do not upload identity documents, health information, passwords or confidential third-party documents.

Purposes and choices

The service uses this information to authenticate you, save private work, display job information, submit an application when you choose, support company hiring workflows, moderate public content, protect account access and answer privacy requests. Guest browsing requires no account. There are no installed analytics, advertising or audience-tracking services, and Nextrole does not sell your information.

Private files and deliberate sharing

Your private résumé library and drafts are readable by your account only through the app’s data policies. You can keep up to 10 library résumés and 50 private versions, each PDF up to 5 MB. On review, choose a specific version. Submitting shares the displayed name, email, cover note and a separate fixed copy of that selected PDF with the company for that job. Other private files and profile fields are not included automatically. Replacing or deleting a library file does not replace an earlier submission. Withdrawal changes status and does not erase the retained submission.

Role access and moderation

Employers can read applications submitted to their company’s jobs and keep separate private hiring notes. Administrators review account metadata, company access, public job content, reports and privacy requests; their default app access does not include candidate application contents, private résumé libraries or employer notes. Report assignments, investigation notes and private resolution reasons are administrator-only. Only an explicitly entered public report message is shared with the reporter and employer. Infrastructure operators may have technical access needed to operate or handle a verified request; such access must be purpose-limited.

Storage, hosting and international processing

Supabase provides authentication, database and private object storage. This project’s configured primary region is ap-northeast-1, Tokyo, Japan, checked on 17 October 2026. The public web deployment uses Netlify hosting. Provider infrastructure, delivery networks, support and operational logs can involve processing outside that primary region. The primary database region is not a guarantee that every provider operation remains in Japan. Provider notices linked below describe their own processing.

Browser storage and file links

Essential browser storage keeps your signed-in session, privacy preference and a selected job to resume after authentication. Optional analytics storage is unused. Private file links generated by the app expire after 60 seconds; anyone who obtains a live link may use it until expiry. Do not share private file links. Public job-sharing links contain no private file link or application payload.

Retention and deletion

The Retention Policy gives concrete review targets. This portfolio uses monthly operator review, not an automatic erasure job. Private drafts become due after 12 months without editing; terminal applications and their snapshots/notes after 6 months from the final status; closed reports and their investigation records after 12 months; resolved privacy requests after 12 months; read notifications after 90 days; audit records after 12 months. Active accounts and private library versions remain available until you remove them, request deletion or the operator reviews whether they are still needed. A longer documented hold may apply to an unresolved dispute, security incident or required record.

Your requests and operator response

Use Privacy & contact for access, correction, export, deletion and retention questions. Signing in protects your request history and replies; this channel remains available to a suspended account with a valid session. The operator targets a response within 30 days and may need to verify account ownership before disclosing or erasing information. This is a service response target, not a claim of compliance with every jurisdiction’s deadlines. You can directly delete private drafts and library files. Account deletion and data export require operator follow-through; a request status alone does not execute them.

Security, provider records and changes

The app uses authenticated requests, row-level ownership policies, private file buckets and expiring links. No service-role secret is included in the web client. No service can promise perfect security. Provider logs and any separately maintained backups follow the operator’s and providers’ controls; deleting an app record does not promise immediate erasure from every provider record. Supabase database backups contain database metadata, not Storage file bytes. Material changes to this notice are published with a new update date.
