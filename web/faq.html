Frequently Asked Questions

Can I browse without signing in?

Yes. Search all sample jobs, use filters, read job details, and save favorites for the current session without an account.

Why do I need an account to apply?

An account keeps your application drafts and résumé associated with you. If you select Apply as a guest, the app asks you to sign in or create an account and then returns you to the selected opportunity.

Are these real vacancies?

This is an internship portfolio demo. Original sample roles never receive applications. The separate employer-listing workspace demonstrates posting, moderation, submission and status tracking using fictional companies and candidate data. No hiring outcome is promised.

Can I use Google sign-in?

Yes. Choose Continue with Google on the account screen, or use an email account. Apple sign-in is not offered.

What happens after I register?

If email confirmation is enabled, open the confirmation link in your email before signing in. If you forget your password, use Forgot password on the sign-in screen.

Where is my résumé stored?

Your private library stores PDFs and their versions. Choose an exact version when reviewing an application; only a separate copy of that version is submitted. Other files stay private, and replacing or deleting a library file does not change prior submissions.

Does this website track me?

No analytics or advertising service is installed. Essential account storage is used only for the requested account functionality.

How do I delete my information?

Delete private drafts and library files from the app. Withdraw submissions from their details. For account deletion, export or retention questions, open Privacy & contact in Settings. Your private request history contains operator replies. Deletion requests require verified operator follow-through.
