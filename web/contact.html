Privacy & Contact

Open private privacy requests

Contact channel

The Nextrole portfolio operator receives privacy questions through the app’s Privacy & contact screen. Select Open private privacy requests below, sign in and send a request. Replies and status changes appear in your request history. No personal email address, public mailbox or external email reply is promised.

What you can request

Choose access, correction, export, deletion, retention or another privacy question. Describe the request in 10–2,000 characters. Do not include passwords, identity documents, health information, confidential documents or another person’s information. Your account and authorized administrators can read the request; other candidates and employers cannot.

Account access

An authenticated session identifies the account and protects request history. If you cannot sign in, use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. A suspended account can still open this privacy channel while it has a valid session. This portfolio does not provide an unauthenticated public support inbox.

Response and follow-through

The operator targets a response within 30 days. A deletion or export request requires review, appropriate ownership checks and server-side follow-through. The app does not claim automatic erasure when a request is submitted or marked resolved.
