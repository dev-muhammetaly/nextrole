Data Retention Policy

Open private privacy requests

How retention is operated

Last updated: 17 October 2026. The operator reviews data at least monthly using the admin Retention review screen and records the review outcome and follow-up in the audit log. The time periods below make records eligible for review. They are not automatic deletion timers. The operator removes or anonymises information that is no longer needed, subject to a documented hold for an unresolved request, dispute, incident or required record.

Accounts, profiles and company information

Keep these while the account and requested service remain in use. Review continued need at least annually and on an account-deletion request. Closing a job or withdrawing an application does not close an account. Account deletion requires verified server administration, including session revocation, dependent data and file handling.

Private drafts and résumé library

Private application drafts are due for review 12 months after their last edit. You may delete them sooner. Library PDFs and their prior private versions remain available while you use the account or until you delete that résumé and its versions. Review continued need annually and on deletion requests. Unfinished uploads and tombstoned metadata require operator housekeeping; deleting a metadata row alone is not file deletion.

Submitted applications and hiring notes

Review a terminal submission 6 months after its status becomes Withdrawn, Rejected or Hired. Review the associated snapshot PDF, contact/cover note, candidate-visible events and employer notes together. An application still being processed remains available for that workflow. Withdrawal or private-file replacement does not delete or mutate the submitted record. Any continued retention after review must have a recorded reason.

Reports and investigation records

Review closed reports, assignments, private investigation notes, resolution summaries and public outcome messages 12 months after resolution or dismissal. Open investigations remain available until resolved or reviewed for continued need. Do not publish private notes as part of a report outcome.

Privacy requests, notifications and audit

Review resolved privacy requests and operator responses after 12 months. Review read in-product notifications after 90 days. Review moderation audit records after 12 months, retaining only what is still needed to explain access or moderation decisions. An audit-review record is not proof that erasure was completed.

Deleting files and handling copies

Private library deletion removes the selected résumé’s object versions through the Storage API before confirming the metadata deletion. Submitted snapshots are separate and require their own retention/deletion process. Operators must delete Storage objects through the Storage API, not merely remove their database metadata. Provider operational logs and any backups or exports are handled under separate provider/operator controls; no fixed provider-wide erasure period is asserted here.

Your choices

Delete your own private files and drafts directly, withdraw an application, or send an in-app privacy request for access, export, correction, deletion or retention questions. The operator targets a response within 30 days and explains any continued retention. This portfolio has no automated bulk erasure, external email notification or public support mailbox.
